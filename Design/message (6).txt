# Purpose

This section intentionally contains ideas that should **not** be prioritized during the MVP, V1, or V2 stages.

These concepts are included because they may become valuable after:

* Large historical datasets exist
* Signals have been validated
* Research infrastructure is mature
* Alpha generation is functioning

Think of these as a long-term research backlog.

# Research Philosophy

Many of these ideas may fail.

That is acceptable.

The objective is not to implement everything.

# Ultimate Endgame

The mature platform should become:

1. A market intelligence system
2. A quantitative research laboratory
3. A prediction platform
4. An event analysis engine
5. A continuously improving alpha discovery system

At that stage, the platform no longer functions merely as an OSRS flipping application.

It functions as a specialized quantitative research environment whose primary dataset happens to be the OSRS economy.

---

# Final Rule

Every future idea must answer:

1. Can it be measured?
2. Can it be backtested?
3. Can it generate alpha?
4. Can it explain itself?
5. Is it better than simpler alternatives?

If the answer is no, the idea remains research only and does not enter production.
