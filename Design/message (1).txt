Build the first institutional-grade market intelligence and quantitative research platform for Old School RuneScape.

# Core Philosophy

Most OSRS trading tools compete on:

* Margins
* Profit calculations
* Price tracking
* Alerts
* Basic recommendations

These are commodity features.

All competitors have access to the same price data.

The real edge comes from:

* Event interpretation
* Community attention
* Historical pattern recognition
* Quantitative research
* Predictive modeling

The goal is therefore not:

"Build a better flip finder"

The goal is:

"Build a Bloomberg Terminal for OSRS."

# Core Trading Engine

## Margin Calculation

Margin

= Sell Price − Buy Price

---

## Net Margin

Net Margin

= Margin − GE Tax

---

## Profit Capacity

Profit Capacity

= Net Margin × Buy Limit

---

## Liquidity Score

Inputs

* Volume
* Spread stability
* Buy limit

Purpose

Avoid illiquid traps.

---

## Base Flip Score

Initial Formula

Score

= Net Margin × log(Liquidity)

This becomes the baseline recommendation engine.

---
