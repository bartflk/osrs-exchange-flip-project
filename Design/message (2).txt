# Objective

# Alternative Data Philosophy

Traditional flipping tools only observe:

* Price
* Volume
* Margins

These are lagging indicators.

This platform will collect leading indicators.

Leading indicators include:

* Community discussion
* Content creator discussion
* Wiki activity
* Poll activity
* Update announcements
* Creator influence
