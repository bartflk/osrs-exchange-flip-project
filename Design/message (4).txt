# OSRS Quant Intelligence Platform (V6)

## Master Specification

### Part 4 — Backtesting Infrastructure, LLM Architecture, Explainability Engine, Recommendation System & Portfolio Intelligence

---

# Objective

Most trading systems fail because they optimize for signal generation instead of signal validation.

Part 4 focuses on proving that signals work.

The primary objective:

Every recommendation generated by the system must be measurable, explainable, and continuously evaluated.

---

# Quantitative Research Philosophy

The platform should operate like a research fund.

Every idea begins as a hypothesis.

Example:

"Attention Velocity predicts future returns."

The platform should automatically answer:

* Does it work?
* How often?
* Under what conditions?
* For which item classes?
* During which market regimes?
