You're thinking in the right direction, but I'd go much further than "Claude summarizes update rumors."

Looking at current OSRS flipping tools, most of them compete on:

Price tracking
Margin finding
Alerts
Charts
Personalized flip recommendations
RuneLite integration
Profit tracking

That's already crowded. Tools like FlipSmart, GE Tracker, and others are already offering AI recommendations, alerts, market flux detection, and analytics.

The opportunity is probably not better dashboards. It's better prediction.

If I were building an OSRS quant platform

I'd build a "market intelligence engine" rather than a flip finder.

Think Renaissance Technologies for RuneScape.

1. Update Impact Forecasting

Instead of:

"Here's today's blog post"

Do:

"These 17 items are likely to rise 5-20% if this proposal passes."

Pipeline:

Monitor Jagex news
Monitor polls
Monitor developer comments
Monitor livestream transcripts
Monitor Reddit discussions
Monitor Discord discussions
Monitor YouTube creator transcripts

Then classify:

Event	Impacted Items	Confidence
New Slayer Boss	Scythe, Blood Fury, Divine pots	72%
Prayer rebalance	Ranarr, Snapdragon	84%
New skilling method	Relevant supplies	91%

This is much more valuable than summarization.

2. Event Similarity Engine

One thing I never see in OSRS tools:

When a new update is announced:

"The closest historical update was Desert Treasure 2."

Then show:

What items moved
How far
How quickly
How long the move lasted

Example:

Project Zanaris announcement

Most similar events:
1. Leagues 4 (83%)
2. Sailing poll (77%)
3. DMM announcement (71%)

Historically:
Teleport items +12%
Bond demand +7%
Skilling resources -4%

This is quant-style analysis.

3. Sentiment Leading Indicator

Reddit is actually useful here.

Track:

Reddit mentions
Wiki page views
YouTube mentions
Discord mentions

Then calculate:

Attention Score
Sentiment Score
Acceleration Score

Often discussion volume rises before price.

Think:

Dragon claws

Price:
flat

Mentions:
+400%

Alert:
"Unusual social activity"

That's how hedge funds track social sentiment.

4. Buy Limit Optimization

Most flippers think:

Margin × Limit

But the real equation is:

Expected Profit =
Margin × Fill Probability × Volume × Time

Create a score:

Expected GP/hour

instead of

Raw Margin

This alone would outperform many current flip finders.

5. Manipulation Detection

Huge opportunity.

Detect:

Fake spikes
Dump groups
Low-volume manipulation
Temporary shortages

Features:

Spread widening
Volume collapse
Abnormal volatility
Trade velocity changes

Output:

WARNING

Elder maul
Price up 18%

Probability of organic move:
21%

Probability of manipulation:
79%
6. Market Regime Detection

This is where quant gets interesting.

Train a model that identifies:

Update speculation market
Panic selling market
Stable market
Bull market
Bear market

Then recommend different strategies.

Example:

Current regime:
Update Speculation

Best strategies:
✓ Momentum
✓ News trades

Avoid:
✗ Long holds
✗ Margin flips
7. Portfolio-Level Optimization

Nobody does this well.

Most tools suggest flips individually.

Instead:

You have:
500m cash
8 GE slots

Optimal allocation:

Slot 1: Dragon claws
Slot 2: Blood fury
Slot 3: Torstol
...
Expected GP/day:
11.4m

Treat GE slots as scarce capital.

This is literally a portfolio optimization problem.

8. Rumor Probability Market

This is the feature I'd personally be excited about.

Create probabilities:

New Raid before Dec:
68%

New Prayer rewards:
42%

New BIS Melee item:
74%

Then map them to assets.

Scythe
Current fair value:
1.82b

If raid confirmed:
2.05b

Expected value:
1.97b

This is much closer to financial markets.

9. "Why Is This Moving?" Engine

A massive pain point.

User sees:

Inquisitor's mace +11%

Your app explains:

Likely causes:

62% New boss proposal
23% Reddit speculation
10% YouTube creator video
5% Unknown

People pay for explanations.

10. Alpha Score (the killer feature)

Combine everything:

Price momentum
Volume acceleration
Spread quality
Social sentiment
Update exposure
Historical analogs

Output:

Top Alpha Opportunities

1. Blood Shard
Score: 91

2. Torstol
Score: 88

3. Dragon Claws
Score: 85

Not "highest margin."

Not "largest volume."

Just:

Which items are statistically most likely to outperform over the next 24h, 7d, or 30d?

That's the feature that starts to feel like Bloomberg Terminal meets OSRS.

My guess is that an AI news/rumor engine is worth building, but only as an input. The real moat is converting news, social discussion, and historical update data into probabilistic forecasts of item prices. Most competitors show what happened. A quant-style product predicts what happens next.
